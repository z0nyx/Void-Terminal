## 1. Scope

Void's source code, documentation, and app-store listing content
(collectively, "the Project") are published publicly at
https://github.com/z0nyx/Void-Terminal. This policy describes how to
report a good-faith claim that material in the Project infringes your
copyright, modeled on the notice-and-takedown process of the U.S.
Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and adapted for a GitHub-hosted
open-source project.

Note that Void's application code and functionality does not host,
serve, or distribute user-generated content of any kind (see the
[Privacy Policy](./privacy.md) and [Acceptable Use Policy](./community-guidelines.md))
— any SSH sessions you run through it exist only between your device and
your chosen host. This policy therefore concerns the Project's own
published materials (source code, assets, documentation, store
listings), not anything transmitted through the App at runtime.

## 2. Filing a Notice of Infringement

If you believe material published as part of the Project infringes a
copyright you own or represent, open an issue on the repository:

**https://github.com/z0nyx/Void-Terminal/issues**

Title it `[DMCA] <short description>` so it is immediately
identifiable. If you would prefer not to file a public issue for a
sensitive matter, note that in the issue title only (e.g.
`[DMCA] Request for private contact`) without including confidential
details in the public issue body, and the Developer will follow up.

## 3. What to Include

To process your notice efficiently, please include:

1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
2. Identification of the specific material in the Project you claim is
   infringing, with enough detail to locate it (a file path, commit, or
   URL within the repository, or the specific store-listing asset).
3. Your contact information.
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use
   is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in
   the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or
   authorized to act on their behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature (a typed full name is
   sufficient in a GitHub issue).

## 4. What Happens Next

The Developer will review a complete notice on a best-effort basis and,
if it is well-founded, remove or disable access to the identified
material from the Project's repository and, where applicable, from
future releases of the App. Given the Project is maintained by a single
independent developer, response time is not guaranteed by SLA but will
be handled promptly on a best-effort basis (see the
[Disclaimer](./disclaimer.md)).

## 5. Counter-Notice

If material you submitted was removed as a result of a mistake or
misidentification, you may file a counter-notice via the same channel
(Section 2), including:

1. Identification of the material that was removed and where it
   previously appeared.
2. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith
   belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or
   misidentification.
3. Your contact information and signature.

## 6. Repeat Infringement

The Developer reserves the right to permanently exclude contributions
from any contributor found to be a repeat copyright infringer with
respect to the Project.
